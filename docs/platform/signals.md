# Signals

Devplan extracts signals from connected meetings, discussions, tickets, and documents. A signal captures a customer problem, request, constraint, or decision with links to its sources, so useful details are easier to find after the original conversation.

Signals are not raw messages or a list of commits. Code changes have their own summaries in [Changelog](/updates).

## Review signals

Open **Knowledge → Signals**, use the available search and filters to find relevant observations, and inspect their source references. Read the original context when the exact wording, speaker, or timing matters.

A signal may capture a customer problem, a request, a constraint, or a decision discussed by the team. It can inform [insights](/insights), proposals, project analysis, and answers from Weaver.

## Interpret the evidence

Check the period covered and whether multiple observations come from the same event. A summarized request is not proof of widespread demand, and a decision discussed in a source is not necessarily an approved commitment.

If expected signals are missing, check the source's configured scope and processing status. Not every source item produces a separate signal.

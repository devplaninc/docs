# Core Workflow

Use Devplan to connect product understanding, planning, and delivery. Start with a useful question, inspect its evidence, and carry what you learn into the next decision.

## Pipeline

| Layer | What it contributes |
|---|---|
| Workspace context | Your product, customers, priorities, and constraints |
| Connected sources | Selected code, tickets, conversations, meeting notes, and documents |
| Product knowledge | Live Docs, code-change summaries, signals, and decisions |
| Analysis | Insights, answers, proposals, reports, and project assessments |
| Your review | Corrections, decisions, and plans grounded in the available evidence |

### 1. Set workspace context

Describe your product, customers, and goals in [Workspace](/knowledge#workspace). Add relevant strategy or research documents and keep important assumptions current.

### 2. Connect integrations

For a new workspace, connect sources through [guided setup](/getting-started) and wait for the initial product map to be ready before entering the main app. After onboarding, use **Integrations** to add or manage sources.

Choose sources that answer your team's questions. For example, code explains what was implemented, while feedback and discussions help explain why it matters. [Integration types](/integrations-overview) differ in whether they process selected material in the background or provide tools Weaver uses when needed.

### 3. Upload documents

Use [uploads](/upload-files) for research or context that is not available through a connected source. Attach project-specific material to the relevant project so its purpose is clear.

### 4. Review product knowledge and insights

Browse [Live Docs](/live-docs), [Changelog](/updates), [signals](/signals), and [decisions](/decisions). Use [Radar](/today) to catch up on the daily report and relevant work, then ask [Weaver](/ask-devplan) to investigate a specific question. For a recurring review, use the [weekly report](/reports) or a [dashboard](/dashboards) focused on your topic.

### 5. Evaluate and plan work

Review the [proposals](/proposals) Devplan creates from connected evidence, or start a [project](/projects) from a problem you already understand. Refine the desired outcome, scope, requirements, and supporting context before moving into implementation.

### 6. Follow delivery

Review project progress and risks against the underlying evidence. Bring project context to a coding assistant through [MCP](/mcp-integration) or the [developer workflow](/spec-driven-development). Keep code and issue trackers current so subsequent analysis reflects the work.

## Typical flow

A customer raises a problem in a meeting. Devplan extracts a signal from the connected notes, relates it to product context, and may surface a feature request or proposal. You inspect the original feedback, refine a project, and follow evidence of its implementation as work progresses.

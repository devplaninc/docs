# Organization Settings

Organizations group workspaces under shared billing and administration. Organization settings are available when your account belongs to an organization with the appropriate permissions.

## General {#general}

Manage organization identity and ownership through organization settings. Organization ownership and workspace membership are different responsibilities; check both when resolving an access problem.

## Workspaces {#workspaces}

Review the workspaces associated with your organization. Adding an existing workspace depends on your permissions and account eligibility, which can include an active subscription or a positive credit balance.

Creating another workspace is a separate capability and may not be available in your account. Joining an existing workspace is usually the right path when your team already has shared context there.

## Billing {#billing}

Use billing settings to review the subscription, credits, usage, and payment-management options available for your organization. Check your billing settings and plan terms for current prices, trial terms, and usage limits.

### Migrating to Organization Billing {#migrate-to-organization-billing}

Create or select the intended organization through your account's billing controls, review its billing arrangement, and associate eligible workspaces with it.

If you already have a personal subscription, review it separately. After creating an organization, check whether your personal subscription is still active. Contact [Devplan](mailto:info@devplan.com) if you need help avoiding overlapping billing.

# Notion

Connect Notion to bring product briefs, research, and team knowledge into Weaver’s answers and planning. Attaching the relevant pages or databases helps preserve the written reasoning behind work alongside its code and tickets.

## Connect and attach

As a workspace Admin, open **Integrations → Notion**, select **Connect Notion**, and authorize access to the intended pages in Notion. Back in Devplan, select **Attach Notion content**, choose accessible pages or databases, and confirm **Attach**. Granting access in Notion and attaching content in Devplan are separate steps.

Review attached content in the management page and remove items that should no longer be included.

## Use the content

Devplan reads attached content during signal processing and can use it in workspace investigations and planning. For example, ask Weaver to relate customer research in Notion to proposed project scope. A connection does not import the entire Notion workspace.

## Troubleshooting

If a page is unavailable, check both its Notion integration access and its attachment in Devplan. If content has moved or permissions have changed, refresh or reconnect as indicated by the integration status.

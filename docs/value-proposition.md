# Why Devplan

Product decisions depend on context spread across code, customer feedback, documents, and team conversations. Devplan helps connect that context so your team can investigate questions, plan work, and follow delivery with supporting evidence.

## Evaluate the value for your team

Choose a recurring task, such as preparing a project review or investigating customer demand. Compare the time needed to reach a useful, verifiable answer and the quality of the evidence before and after connecting relevant sources.

Results depend on your source coverage, configuration, and workflow. See [Getting Started](/getting-started) to begin with a focused task.
